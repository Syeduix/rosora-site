Private ADHD Assessment Costs: What You Need to Know

By the Rosora team  •  5 min read

When choosing a private ADHD assessment, it is important to look beyond the advertised price. Assessment fees can vary depending on the clinician involved, the assessment process, the information collected and what is included after your appointment.

What should an ADHD assessment include?

A comprehensive ADHD assessment should consider more than symptoms alone. At Rosora, the assessment process may include:

Pre-assessment questionnaires
A detailed clinical and developmental history
Exploration of ADHD symptoms in childhood and adulthood
Assessment of how symptoms affect everyday functioning
Relevant information from a family member, partner, school or other informant where appropriate
Mental and physical health history
Consideration of other possible or co-existing conditions
A structured ADHD diagnostic interview
Clinical formulation and diagnostic decision
Feedback about the outcome
A comprehensive written report

What does the assessment fee cover?

Before booking, you should know exactly what is included in the price. Rosora aims to provide clear information about:

What is included within your assessment
Whether your diagnostic report is included
Whether further information may be required
Any separate medication or treatment costs
Follow-up options after your assessment

Assessment and medication treatment are separate clinical services and any additional charges will be explained clearly.

Does paying for an assessment guarantee an ADHD diagnosis?

No.

Paying for an assessment gives you access to a professional diagnostic assessment. It does not guarantee a diagnosis.

The purpose of the assessment is to determine whether the clinical evidence supports ADHD after considering your developmental history, symptoms, functional difficulties and other possible explanations.

Possible outcomes may include:

1ADHD diagnostic criteria are met
2ADHD diagnostic criteria are not met
3Further information is required before a decision can be reached

Whatever the outcome, we will explain the findings and recommended next steps.

Choosing an ADHD assessment

When comparing providers, consider:

The qualifications and experience of the clinician
The depth of the assessment
Whether childhood and current symptoms are considered
Whether functional impairment is assessed
Whether other conditions are considered
Whether informant information is used where appropriate
Whether a written report is included
What support is available following the assessment

At Rosora Autism and ADHD Assessment Practice, our aim is to provide a clear, thorough and person-centred assessment pathway with transparent information about what you are paying for.
