Medication

Titration & Monitoring

Finding the right dose takes time. Titration is the process of adjusting your medication gradually, with regular review, to find what works best for you.

Start Your JourneyAsk a Question

What to expect during titration

Your prescriber will start at an appropriate dose and review your response at agreed intervals, adjusting gradually based on how you're responding and any side effects.

Once a stable, effective dose is reached, monitoring continues on an ongoing basis to make sure the medication continues to work well for you over time.
