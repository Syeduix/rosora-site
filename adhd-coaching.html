Treatment

ADHD Coaching That Works With You

ADHD coaching at Rosora is practical, personalised, and focused on making everyday life feel more manageable.

Enquire Now

Rather than trying to change who you are, coaching helps you understand how your ADHD affects you, identify what gets in the way, recognise your strengths and develop strategies that work for your life.

The aim is to help you build greater clarity, confidence and consistency while developing strategies you can continue using long after coaching sessions have ended.

Together, we can explore areas such as:

  • Organisation
  • Procrastination
  • Time management
  • Motivation
  • Emotional regulation
  • Confidence
  • Relationships
  • Achieving personal or professional goals
Personal to you

Coaching designed around your ADHD

ADHD can affect people very differently.

You may know exactly what you need to do but struggle to get started. You may lose track of time, become overwhelmed by competing priorities, act impulsively or find it difficult to maintain routines.

For others, ADHD may affect confidence, emotional wellbeing, relationships, work or study.

Rosora coaching begins with your priorities.

We work collaboratively to understand what is happening, what you would like to change and what practical strategies are most likely to work for you.

There is no one-size-fits-all coaching programme.

Our approach

The Rosora coaching approach

Our ADHD coaching combines neurodiversity-informed coaching with practical strategies for everyday functioning. Your coaching may include:

01

Understanding Your ADHD

Develop a clearer understanding of your individual ADHD profile, including your strengths, challenges, patterns, triggers and support needs.

02

Executive Function Strategies

Develop practical approaches to:

  • Planning and prioritising
  • Starting and completing tasks
  • Organisation
  • Working memory
  • Time management
  • Maintaining attention
  • Managing competing demands
03

Breaking Through Procrastination

Explore what drives avoidance or difficulty getting started, and develop realistic strategies to build momentum.

Instead of relying solely on willpower, we look at ways to make tasks more achievable, visible and rewarding.

04

Emotional Regulation

Learn to recognise emotional patterns, identify triggers and develop strategies for responding to frustration, overwhelm, rejection, stress or impulsive reactions more effectively.

05

Goal Setting and Accountability

Turn ideas and intentions into manageable actions.

Together, we can break larger goals into realistic steps, review progress, and adapt strategies when something isn't working.

06

Building Confidence

ADHD can sometimes affect how people see themselves, particularly after years of struggling with expectations that did not reflect how they think and function.

Coaching can help you recognise strengths, challenge unhelpful patterns, and build confidence in your ability to manage everyday life.

07

Creating Sustainable Routines

Rather than expecting you to follow rigid systems, we help you develop routines and structures that fit your lifestyle, responsibilities, and ADHD needs.

Work & career

ADHD coaching for work and career

ADHD can bring valuable strengths to the workplace, including creativity, energy, problem-solving, innovation and the ability to think differently.

However, difficulties with organisation, deadlines, prioritisation, meetings, workload or maintaining attention can sometimes create additional pressure.

Where appropriate, coaching may also complement support provided through Access to Work.

Rosora coaching can help you develop strategies around:

Workload management
Prioritising tasks
Meeting deadlines
Managing distractions
Preparing for meetings
Communication
Workplace confidence
Career planning
Reasonable adjustments
Managing transitions or changes at work
Preventing overwhelm and burnout
Relationships

ADHD and relationships

ADHD can also influence communication and relationships.

Differences in attention, emotional regulation, impulsivity, memory, and organisation can sometimes lead to misunderstandings between partners, family members, friends, or colleagues.

Coaching can help you understand these patterns and develop practical approaches to communication, boundaries, shared responsibilities and relationship wellbeing.

A strengths-based approach

Progress, not perfection

At Rosora, ADHD coaching is not about correcting your personality or expecting you to become someone different.

It is about understanding how your brain works, recognising what already works well and developing strategies for the areas where you would like more support.

We focus on progress, not perfection. The goal is to help you create systems that work with you, not against you.

The process

Your coaching journey

Your coaching begins by identifying what matters most to you. Together, we can:

  1. 1

    Understand

    Explore your ADHD experiences, strengths, challenges and recurring patterns.

  2. 2

    Prioritise

    Identify the areas that would make the biggest difference to your daily life.

  3. 3

    Plan

    Create realistic goals and practical strategies.

  4. 4

    Experiment

    Try different approaches and discover what works best for you.

  5. 5

    Review

    Reflect on progress and adjust strategies when needed.

  6. 6

    Build Independence

    Develop tools, confidence, and self-awareness you can keep using beyond coaching.

Start where you are

Coaching that is personal to you

You do not need to arrive with everything figured out.

You may have one specific challenge you want to work on, or you may simply know that everyday life feels harder than it needs to. We start from where you are and build from there.
