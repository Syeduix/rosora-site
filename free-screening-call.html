What a Rosora screening call can help you decide

Understand whether assessment is the right next step

The screening call gives you the opportunity to discuss your main concerns, current difficulties and what has led you to consider an Autism or ADHD assessment.

We can help you understand whether a formal neurodevelopmental assessment may be appropriate, or whether another pathway may be more suitable.

Choose the right pathway

Sometimes it is not immediately clear whether your difficulties are more consistent with ADHD, Autism, or a combination of both. Your screening call can help guide you towards the most appropriate pathway:

  • ADHD Assessment
  • Autism Assessment
  • Combined Autism + ADHD Assessment

This can help you avoid booking an assessment pathway that may not be the best fit for your needs.

Clarify your next steps

Ask what matters to you

You can use the screening call to ask questions about:

What the assessment involves
Which assessment pathway may be most appropriate
What forms or questionnaires may be required
Whether informant information will be helpful
What documents you may need to provide
What happens after assessment
Medication options where relevant
Post-diagnostic support and coaching
