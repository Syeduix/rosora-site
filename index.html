Who We Are

Assessment that considers the whole person

We carry out our assessments in line with relevant NICE guidance and recognised diagnostic standards, using a comprehensive, evidence-informed approach that considers the whole person, not just scores on questionnaires or screening tools.

We take time to understand developmental history, current experiences, strengths, challenges and the impact of symptoms or characteristics across everyday life, including home, education, work, relationships and social settings. Where appropriate, we also consider information from parents, carers, partners, schools, or other informants as part of the assessment.

We also consider alternative explanations and coexisting conditions to support careful clinical formulation and diagnostic decision-making.

Your experience

What you can expect

From your first enquiry through assessment, diagnostic feedback, and recommendations, our aim is to provide a clear, compassionate, person-centred experience.

Where diagnostic criteria are met, you will receive:

  • 1A clear diagnostic outcome
  • 2A comprehensive clinical report
  • 3Personalised recommendations to help you understand the next steps
