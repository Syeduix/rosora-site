Medication Support

If medication may help following an ADHD diagnosis, we make the pathway clear from the outset — including what Rosora does and doesn't do.

Start Your JourneyAsk a Question
Important: Rosora does not hold or dispense medication directly, and shared-care arrangements are not currently available through Rosora. Medication is prescribed and monitored via your nominated pharmacy and prescriber, following the pathway below.

The Medication Pathway

Diagnosis→Medication Assessment→Prescriber Review→Treatment Decision→Prescription→Nominated Pharmacy→Titration & Monitoring→Ongoing Review
